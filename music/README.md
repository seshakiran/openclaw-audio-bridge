# 🎵 Background Music

Place `.mp3` files in this directory. They'll play randomly as background music while OpenClaw processes your request.

## Recommended Sources (royalty-free)

- [Pixabay Music](https://pixabay.com/music/) — Free for commercial use
- [Free Music Archive](https://freemusicarchive.org/) — CC-licensed tracks
- [Uppbeat](https://uppbeat.io/) — Free with attribution

## Tips

- Use ambient/lo-fi tracks (they work best as background)
- Keep files under 10MB each
- 3-5 tracks is plenty for variety
